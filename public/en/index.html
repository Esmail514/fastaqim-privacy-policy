01

Scope and contact

Who is responsible for your data?

The Fastaqim app developer is responsible. Contact support.fastaqim@gmail.com with questions or privacy requests. This policy covers the app, connected features and policy website.

The current app requires no account or login and contains no ads, usage analytics or automatic crash-reporting SDK. It does not automatically upload worship progress or personal notes. Particular features contact the external services below.

02

Storage on your device

What is stored in the app?

Fastaqim uses local databases and preferences to remember:

  • Appearance, font size, Quran narration, prayer calculation method, audio and notification settings.
  • Quran bookmarks, highlights, notes, categories, reading position and khatm plans and progress.
  • Daily prayer, Quran and adhkar tracking, streaks, tasbih counters, custom adhkar and their history.
  • Your selected location or last saved coordinates, their timestamp, and city or country when available.
  • Downloaded content and audio, and information needed to resume downloads.

There is no cloud sync. Your system may manage backups or device transfers. Local storage does not imply special file encryption or exclusion from system backups.

03

Prayer times, Qibla and mosques

How is location used?

Approximate or precise location, depending on permission, supports local prayer-time and Qibla calculations. You can choose a city or map point instead. If no suitable location is available, the app may temporarily use Makkah and show a notice.

Android's geocoding service may identify the country from coordinates; network use depends on the device provider. Mosque search sends coordinates and search radius to Overpass and may start when you open that section with permission granted. City or address searches send the entered text to Nominatim. Map-tile requests reveal the viewed area to OpenStreetMap. Opening directions passes the mosque destination to your chosen maps app.

04

Connections used by the feature

Content downloads and audio

Requests for hadith books, tafsir and surah introductions contact content hosted on Supabase. These download requests retrieve content; they do not upload your notes, bookmarks or worship history.

The reciter catalogue uses MP3Quran, with full recitations from its associated audio servers. Verse-by-verse playback uses EveryAyah. Adhkar audio comes from Archive.org and GitHub Raw. Streaming and downloads send file requests and ordinary connection data, such as your IP address, to the provider. On Android, requested downloads can continue in the background with a progress notification and locally saved task state. You can cancel transfers or delete downloads in storage management.

05

Information you choose to send

Reports, suggestions and corrections

Submitting a technical report or suggestion sends its category, title, description, app version, operating-system information and submission time to Supabase. Contact details are included only if you enter them. A correction sends the section, original text and proposed replacement, plus evidence, notes or contact details you add.

These messages support review of issues, suggestions and corrections. Submitting a correction does not directly change published content. Avoid passwords, identity documents and unnecessary personal details. If you contact the developer by email, the message and sender address are handled through the email service you use.

06

Why the app uses them

Permissions and device services

  • Location: prayer times, Qibla and mosque search, with manual alternatives.
  • Notifications and exact alarms: prayer, adhkar and Quran reminders, with controls for their categories.
  • Background playback and downloads: continued audio and requested transfers, with controls and progress.
  • Vibration, wake services and boot handling: interaction, feature operation and restoring reminder schedules.

The current app requests no contacts, camera, microphone or photo access, and no separate background-location permission. Permission names and controls vary by operating system. Local notifications may appear on the lock screen according to your device settings; those settings control whether their content is visible.

07

Who receives connection requests?

External services

External providers receive data needed for the feature request and may maintain connection logs under their own policies and settings:

  • Supabase: content hosting, reports and corrections.
  • OpenStreetMap Foundation: privacy information for its services; some Overpass servers have independent operators.
  • MP3Quran, EveryAyah and Archive.org: audio catalogues and files.
  • GitHub: audio files and website hosting when used.
  • Cloudflare: website services according to the hosting configuration.

Providers may process requests outside your country; server locations and log-retention periods are not specified here. Their notices do not replace Fastaqim's explanation of its practices.

08

Current retention practice

Retention and deletion limits

Local data remains until changed, removed through available tools or cleared through device settings. Uninstallation and system backups depend on your device and its configuration.

Submitted reports and corrections are not currently deleted. The developer retains them for later review without a defined retention period. There is no automatic deletion process or in-app server-deletion control. You can email about submitted data or a privacy request, without an automatic-deletion promise or stated deadline. External logs and backups depend on provider settings. Local deletion does not withdraw a sent message.

09

Actions available to you

Your controls and choices

You can refuse or revoke location permission, use manual alternatives, disable reminders or their categories, and choose not to submit feedback. You can remove Quran bookmarks, highlights and notes, delete custom adhkar, and remove downloaded content and audio through storage management.

Resetting settings restores preferences, without erasing every record. Clearing the image cache leaves notes, progress and audio intact. Use device data-clearing tools for all local app data; review backups separately. Sharing content passes it to your chosen recipient, whose practices apply. No account is needed.

10

Children and guardians

Use by all age groups

Fastaqim is for all ages, including children, without birthdays or accounts. Reading and adhkar need no name or contact details. Guardians should help children configure permissions and connected features and decide what to share.

Children should avoid personal information in reports or corrections and describe only the issue. A guardian can contact support.fastaqim@gmail.com about a child's message or data. Availability to all ages does not mean external services have identical age requirements; their terms may differ.

11

Read without signing up

This website and policy updates

This static policy website supports Arabic and English. It contains no data-collection forms, ads, added trackers or analytics, and creates no cookies. After you choose a theme, localStorage remembers that display preference in your browser. Clearing site data removes it.

When hosted on GitHub Pages or through Cloudflare, hosting providers may receive IP addresses, request data and operational or security logs according to the service configuration. The email button opens your email app. We update this text when relevant practices change. “Last updated” identifies this version's review date and does not mean the website was published then.
